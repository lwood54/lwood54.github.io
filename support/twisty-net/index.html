Get in touch

Email lwoodapps@gmail.com. I'm one person, so a reply can take a few days. For a bug, it helps to include your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

How do I play?

Tap a Tile to turn it. Power flows from the Source in the middle along every wire that meets another. Turn the Tiles until every one is lit, with no loose ends and no loops, and the puzzle is solved. Sure of a Tile? Press and hold it to lock it, so it can't turn by mistake. Settings → How to play runs the tutorial again.

Tokens and Hints

  • A new puzzle costs one Token. You get 3 free Tokens and 3 free Hints each day, topped up at midnight.
  • Need more? Tap Get more beside your Token and Hint counts to watch an optional video for 3 more. Videos are only ever shown when you choose to watch one.
  • Tokens you earn from videos never expire, and 30 of them unlock a Theme.
  • Videos need an internet connection. Everything else works offline.

Remove Ads, and restoring it

  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase: no ads, unlimited puzzles and Hints, and every Theme. It's shared with your Family Sharing group.
  • New phone, or reinstalled the app? Open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page. Purchases go through Apple, so I can't refund them directly.

Your progress and privacy

  • There are no accounts. Your progress, stats and Theme unlocks stay on your device and in your device backups.
  • Deleting the app deletes your progress. Remove Ads isn't lost: restore it as above.
  • Where the law gives you a choice about ads, Settings → Privacy options lets you change it.
  • The full details are in the privacy policy.
