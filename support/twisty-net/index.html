Support

Get in touch

Email lwoodapps@gmail.com. I'm one person, so a reply can take a few days. For a bug, it helps to include your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

How do I play?

Tap a Tile to turn it. Power flows from the Source in the middle along every wire that meets another. Turn the Tiles until every one is lit, with no loose ends and no loops, and the puzzle is solved. Sure of a Tile? Press and hold it to lock it, so it can't turn by mistake. Settings → How to play runs the tutorial again.

Puzzles and Hints

  • Each new puzzle uses one of your puzzles. You get 5 free puzzles and 3 free Hints each day, topped up at midnight, plus 10 puzzles to start.
  • Need more? Tap Get more beside your puzzle and Hint counts to watch an optional video for 3 more. Videos are only ever shown when you choose to watch one.
  • Videos need an internet connection. Everything else works offline.

The Daily

  • One new puzzle every day, the same for everyone, from the Daily button on the home screen. It's Easy on Monday and climbs to Expert on Sunday.
  • You get one try, and the clock runs while you play. Solve it on consecutive days to build your streak. A new Daily starts at midnight UTC.

Themes

  • Electricity and Neon are free. Magma unlocks after 25 solved puzzles, and Radioactive after playing 5 days in a row. Prism is a one-time purchase.
  • Choose a Theme from Themes on the home screen.

Game Center

  • Leaderboards for every size and difficulty and for the Daily, plus achievements, from Leaderboards on the home screen.
  • Game Center is optional. To sign in, use your iPhone's Settings → Game Center. If you're signed in, Themes you've earned come back on a new device.

Remove Ads, Themes, and restoring purchases

  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase: no ads, and unlimited puzzles and Hints. Prism is a separate one-time purchase. Both are shared with your Family Sharing group.
  • New phone, or reinstalled the app? Open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page. Purchases go through Apple, so I can't refund them directly.

Your progress and privacy

  • No account needed. Your progress, stats and Themes stay on your device and in your device backups.
  • Deleting the app deletes your progress. Purchases aren't lost: restore them as above.
  • Where the law gives you a choice about ads, Settings → Privacy options lets you change it.
  • The full details are in the privacy policy.
